The four forces working against you

01
Glaciers are dying
Glacier NP loses its glaciers by ~2030. Rainier, North Cascades, and AK glaciers visibly retreat each year.
02
Alaska is for the young
Most AK parks are fly-in, no trails, grizzly country. Costly and physical. Easier at 32 than 52.
03
Permits keep tightening
Half Dome, Mt. Whitney, Wonderland, The Wave, Angels Landing — all lottery now, getting worse.
04
Reefs degrading fast
Dry Tortugas, Virgin Islands, Biscayne, American Samoa coral systems decline annually.
Tier 01 · Do Now Now → 35 · 15 parks

The do-it-now-or-regret-it parks.

Hardest, most fragile, most remote. These reward youth and punish delay — multi-day backcountry, 14,000-foot summits, fly-in-only Alaska, glaciers that won't be here in 20 years.

The Alaska 8 — bundle into 2-3 trips
Gates of the Arctic AK
No trails, no campsites. Bushwhack across the Brooks Range.
Kobuk Valley AK
Fly-in. Arctic sand dunes. ~15K visitors/year.
Lake Clark AK
Floatplane only. Bear viewing at Silver Salmon Creek.
Katmai AK
Brooks Falls bears. Valley of 10,000 Smokes backpack.
Wrangell–St. Elias AK
Largest US park (6 Yellowstones). Root Glacier ice climbing.
Glacier Bay AK
Kayak among calving tidewater glaciers. Retreating each year.
Kenai Fjords AK
Harding Icefield trek. Glacier paddling.
Denali AK
Backcountry permit zones. No trails. Summit if alpine-trained.
Glaciers dying — go before they're gone
Glacier MT
Highline Trail, Grinnell Glacier, Iceberg Lake. Glaciers projected gone by 2030. 2026
North Cascades WA
Most rugged park in the Lower 48. Cascade Pass, Sahale Arm, Boston Basin.
Mount Rainier WA
Wonderland Trail (93mi loop) or Camp Muir push to high camp.
Permit lotteries & leg-killers
Yosemite CA
Half Dome cables, Clouds Rest, High Sierra Camp loop, Cathedral Lakes.
Sequoia / Kings Canyon CA
Mt. Whitney summit (14,505ft), Rae Lakes Loop, High Sierra Trail.
Isle Royale MI
Backpack-only park. Ferry/seaplane in. Greenstone Ridge 40-mile traverse.
American Samoa AS
Most remote NP. Reefs degrading. Jungle hikes. Homestay culture.

Strong Opinion

Your next three years.

SF-based, EV sedan, early 30s — the entire West is in striking distance. Here's how I'd sequence the next three years to maximize the Tier 1 window.

2026 · This Year
Yellowstone + Glacier
Yellowstone in May (booked). Add Glacier NP late July or August — it's the #1 "do it now" park in the Lower 48. Pair with Grand Teton on the drive back.
2027 · The Big One
Alaska, two weeks
Denali + Wrangell–St. Elias + Kenai Fjords. ~$8–10K and physically demanding, worth doing once at full capacity. Knock out 3 of the 8 Alaska parks.
2028 · High Sierra
Yosemite + Sequoia
Half Dome lottery (March) + Mt. Whitney lottery (Feb). Bookend with Lassen on the drive. The High Sierra at full strength is a peak life experience.
