Hidden Gems & Free Activities

SoCal Road Trip  ·  Apr 17–20, 2026  ·  Beyond the Tourist Trail
Along the Drive Fri Apr 17 & Mon Apr 20
Piedras Blancas Elephant Seal Rookery
FREE · 20 min
Hundreds of massive elephant seals lounging on the beach — wild, loud, and unforgettable. Elevated boardwalk viewing area right off the highway. One of the most surprising stops on the CA coast. Parents will be amazed.
Hwy 1, San Simeon (7 mi north of Hearst Castle) — ~20 min detour off 101 near SLO
Best as a quick stop before or after SLO lunch on Day 1. Add ~40 min total to your drive (detour + viewing)
Morro Rock
FREE · 10 min
A massive 581-ft volcanic plug jutting out of the ocean. Dramatic, almost surreal. Quick drive-by photo or walk to the base. Morro Bay itself is a cute harbor town.
Morro Rock, Morro Bay — off Hwy 1, ~15 min north of SLO
Combine with elephant seals for a Hwy 1 mini-loop before heading to SLO. Or stop on the way south after SLO
Bubblegum Alley
FREE · 5 min
An entire 70-ft alley in downtown SLO covered floor-to-ceiling in chewed gum. Disgusting and delightful. Great photo op. Right off Higuera St near your lunch spot.
733 Higuera St, San Luis Obispo
Casa de Fruta Quirky Stops
$5–10 · 30 min
Old-school roadside attraction on Hwy 152 (Mon drive home). Mini narrow-gauge train ride, carousel, gold panning, duck pond, fruit stand, and a candy store. Kitschy and fun — perfect last-stop energy.
10031 Pacheco Pass Hwy, Hollister
Los Angeles Gems Sat Apr 18
Eaton Canyon Falls Hike
FREE · 2 hrs
A real waterfall, 15 min from your Alhambra Airbnb! 3.5 mi round trip along a shaded canyon. Moderate difficulty — rocky in spots but mostly flat until the end. The 40-ft waterfall at the end is the payoff. Best done early morning before crowds.
Eaton Canyon Natural Area Park, 1750 N Altadena Dr, Pasadena
Perfect Sat 7–9am activity before Huntington Gardens. Wear sturdy shoes. Trailhead parking fills up by 10am on weekends
The Last Bookstore
FREE · 20–30 min
A massive bookstore inside an old bank vault in DTLA. Upstairs has a tunnel made entirely of books, art installations built from pages, and a labyrinth of shelves. One of the most Instagrammed spots in LA — but it lives up to the hype.
453 S Spring St, Los Angeles (DTLA)
Pair with Grand Central Market lunch (5 min walk) and Angels Flight ($1, next block)
Angels Flight Railway
$1/ride · 5 min
“Shortest railway in the world” — a tiny funicular built in 1901 that climbs a steep hill in Bunker Hill. Featured in La La Land. Right next to Grand Central Market.
350 S Grand Ave, Los Angeles (DTLA)
Venice Canals
FREE · 20 min
Walk 2 blocks inland from the Venice Boardwalk chaos and you hit actual canals with charming houses, arched bridges, and ducks. Feels like a secret neighborhood. Most tourists have no idea this exists.
Venice Canal Historic District, Venice Beach — enter from Dell Ave & S Venice Blvd
Pair with Santa Monica Pier + Venice Boardwalk afternoon. Walk south on the boardwalk, then cut inland to the canals
Bradbury Building
FREE · 5 min
Step inside the lobby of this 1893 building and look up — ornate wrought-iron railings, open cage elevators, a skylit atrium. Featured in Blade Runner and 500 Days of Summer. Stunning architecture hidden behind a plain facade.
304 S Broadway, Los Angeles (DTLA)
2 blocks from The Last Bookstore — do both in one 30-min DTLA walk
San Diego Gems Sun Apr 19 & Mon Apr 20
Chicano Park Murals
FREE · 15–20 min
The largest collection of outdoor murals in the world, painted on the massive pillars under the Coronado Bridge. Vivid, powerful Chicano art — a National Historic Landmark. Right next to your Logan Heights Airbnb. Walk here before heading out Sun morning or Mon before Coronado.
1949 Logan Ave, San Diego (under the Coronado Bridge, Logan Heights)
Best in morning light. It's literally a 5-min walk from your Airbnb — don't skip this
Balboa Park Free Organ Concert
FREE · 30–45 min
Every Sunday at 2:00 PM, one of the world's largest outdoor pipe organs plays a free concert at the Spruce Street Organ Pavilion. Sit in the open amphitheater surrounded by Balboa Park gardens. A magical, only-in-SD experience.
Spruce Street Organ Pavilion, Balboa Park — south end of the park near the Palisades area
Fits perfectly between Balboa Park exploration (11am) and heading to USS Midway (2:30pm). Grab a seat by 1:50pm
Torrey Pines Gliderport
FREE to watch · 15–20 min
Paragliders and hang gliders launch off 300-ft ocean cliffs and soar on the thermals. Sit on the bluff and watch them fly — mesmerizing. Dramatic coastal views north and south. Tandem paraglide flights ~$175/pp if feeling adventurous (no experience needed).
2800 Torrey Pines Scenic Dr, La Jolla
On your route between La Jolla kayak and Sunset Cliffs. Stop for 15 min, or book a tandem flight for the adrenaline rush of a lifetime
Spruce Street Suspension Bridge
FREE · 5 min
A wooden pedestrian suspension bridge spanning a canyon in the Bankers Hill neighborhood. It bounces as you walk — slightly thrilling, surprisingly scenic. Hidden in a residential area that most visitors never find.
Spruce St & Front St, San Diego (Bankers Hill — near Balboa Park)
5 min detour from Balboa Park. Walk to the middle, look down into the eucalyptus canyon
Sunset Cliffs Tide Pools
FREE · 20 min
You're already going to Sunset Cliffs — but at low tide, the rocky shelves below reveal tide pools full of anemones, crabs, and small fish. Check the tide chart before going: you need a low tide below 1.0 ft.
Sunset Cliffs Natural Park, Ladera St stairs, Point Loma
Check tides at tides.net for Apr 20. If low tide aligns with sunset, you hit the jackpot
Events This Weekend + Bonus Apr 17–20, 2026
LA Times Festival of Books
FREE · Sat–Sun
The nation's largest literary/cultural festival at USC. 700+ exhibitor booths, author panels, book signings, food vendors, live music. ~150K attendees. Sarah Jessica Parker, Larry David, Lionel Richie among guests. Sat 10am–6pm, Sun 10am–5pm.
USC University Park Campus, 3551 Trousdale Pkwy, Los Angeles
Sat afternoon fits well after Hollywood. Gets packed — go early or late. Free but some panels need ticket reservations
Grand Prix of Long Beach
$58–88 · Fri–Sun
IndyCar, IMSA sports cars, Formula Drift, Stadium Super Trucks — all racing through downtown Long Beach. 190K fans. Food trucks, car show (Ferraris, Porsches), Family Fun Zone. Main IndyCar race Sun 2:45pm.
300 E Ocean Blvd, Long Beach
Friday GA is cheapest ($58). Long Beach is ~30 min from Alhambra. Great if anyone in the family likes cars/racing
Day at the Docks — San Diego
FREE · Sun 9a–5p
West Coast's largest sportfishing celebration. Kid's fishing adventure (ages 4–12), $5 boat rides around SD Bay, seafood/Thai/Mexican food vendors, live music. Fun & free at Point Loma waterfront.
Fisherman's Landing, 2838 Garrison St, Point Loma, San Diego
Free shuttles from Shelter Island parking. On the way to Sunset Cliffs — stop by if timing works Sun afternoon
Global Earth Day Festival — Balboa Park
FREE · Sun 11am+
WorldBeat Cultural Center's 34th annual celebration. Live music, art, environmental exhibits, community vibes. Right inside Balboa Park — you'll walk past it on Sunday.
WorldBeat Cultural Center, 2100 Park Blvd, Balboa Park
Smorgasburg LA
FREE entry · Sun 10a–4p
Brooklyn-born open-air food market with dozens of vendors. Great for grazing. But you'll be driving to SD on Sunday morning — only works if you delay departure.
ROW DTLA, 777 Alameda St, Los Angeles
Tandem Paragliding — Torrey Pines
~$175/pp · 25 min
Run off a 300-ft cliff and soar over the Pacific with an instructor. No experience needed. The most exhilarating thing in SD. Book at flytorrey.com. Weather-dependent.
2800 Torrey Pines Scenic Dr, La Jolla
Windansea Beach + Cabrillo Monument
FREE / $20/car
Windansea: Local's La Jolla beach, dramatic rocks, surf shack (6800 Neptune Pl). Cabrillo: Tip of Point Loma, panoramic views, lighthouse, tide pools (1800 Cabrillo Memorial Dr). Both near Sunset Cliffs route.
